## 3) Compliance-driven design constraints (HMRC)
Implement the following gates and invoice requirements:

### 3.1 Self-billing agreement gate
Before a self-billed invoice can be issued:
- supplier must have an **active, non-expired** self-billing agreement (VAT Notice 700/62). :contentReference[oaicite:19]{index=19}
- agreement must be stored and retrievable (for audit pack).
- agreement should be reviewable/renewable (annual review is common; do not hardcode unless already in code).

### 3.2 VAT / rate handling (no hard-coded 20%)
- Today the system assumes 20% VAT by default. This is **not universally correct**. :contentReference[oaicite:20]{index=20}
- Implement a **VAT profile** for supplier (and optionally per service type) that supports:
  - VAT registered: yes/no
  - VAT number (required if VAT registered)
  - VAT rate selection for invoices (default should be configurable; rates depend on supply type). :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21}
- If supplier is **not VAT registered**, system must generate invoices with **no VAT** (0 VAT amount).

### 3.3 Self-billed :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22}lf-billed invoice PDFs must contain:
- Supplier name/address/VAT number (if VAT registered) + Buyer name/address/VAT number (if VAT registered) + invoice number + invoice date + line descriptions + totals.
- Required statement: **“The VAT shown is your output tax due to HMRC”**. :contentReference[oaicite:23]{index=23}

### 3.4 Record keeping / audit
- Keep VAT records and supporting documents with a retention approach consistent with VAT record keeping guidance. :contentReference[oaicite:24]{index=24}

  - who approved a timesheet, when, what changed
